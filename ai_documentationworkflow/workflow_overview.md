# Workflow Overview

## Purpose

This workflow illustrates how AI can support documentation activities while keeping technical writers and subject matter experts responsible for technical accuracy, content quality, and approval.

---

## High-Level Workflow

```text
Requirements / Feature Inputs
            │
            ▼
     AI Draft Generation
            │
            ▼
 Technical Writer Review
            │
            ▼
      SME Validation
            │
            ▼
         Publishing
            │
            ▼
 Continuous Improvement
```

---

## Workflow Principles

* AI assists with content creation and review.
* Technical writers ensure end-user experience, clarity, usability, and accuracy.
* Subject matter experts validate technical correctness.
* Published content remains under human ownership.
* Feedback and usage insights drive continuous improvement.

---

## Expected Benefits

* Faster content development
* Improved content consistency
* Reduced repetitive authoring tasks
* Increased focus on strategic documentation work
