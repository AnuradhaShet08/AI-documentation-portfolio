# Governance Principles

## Purpose

These principles help you ensure that AI-assisted documentation remains accurate, trustworthy, and aligned with organizational standards.



## Human Review Required

AI-generated content should always be reviewed before publication.

Human reviewers remain accountable for final content quality.



## Accuracy Validation

Technical information should be validated against source materials and product requirements.

AI output should not be treated as an authoritative source.



## Content Ownership

Documentation teams retain ownership of all published content.

AI acts as a supporting tool rather than a content owner.



## Compliance with Standards

AI-assisted content should adhere to:

* Style guides
* Terminology standards
* Documentation guidelines
* Regulatory requirements where applicable



## Transparency

Documentation teams should understand where AI is used within the content lifecycle and apply appropriate review controls.



## Continuous Improvement

AI workflows should be regularly evaluated to ensure they continue to meet quality, usability, and business objectives.
