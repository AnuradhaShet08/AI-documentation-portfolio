# When to Use this Guide

Use this guide to correct errors in your Experian Credit Report such as:

- Incorrect personal details  
- Incorrect account information  
- Incorrect enquiries

---

## Audience

This guide is intended for:

- Consumers who want to correct errors in their Experian Credit Report  
- Users raising disputes related to personal, account, or enquiry information  
- First time users navigating the dispute process  
- Existing Experian users tracking or managing disputes

---

# Before You Get Started

## Prerequisites

Ensure you have the following information handy:

- Access to your Experian Credit Report.

- **ERN** (Experian Report Number)  
  You can find it at the top right corner of your Experian Credit Report.

- **UTI** (Unique Transaction ID)  
  A globally unique identifier for your individual transactions. You can find it in the email body sent by Experian Credit View.

> **Note:** If ERN and UTI are older than 30 days, you must fetch a fresh report to raise a dispute.

# Access the Dispute Page

Choose one of the following methods.

## Important
> If you recently made payments, wait 30 days for changes such as foreclosures, payments, or closures to reflect in your report.

---

## Option A: Use ERN and UTI

1. Navigate to the dispute page link.

2. On the dispute form page, enter:
   - Experian Reference Number (ERN)
   - Unique Transaction ID (UTI)

3. Click **Verify**.

Insert screenshot: Dispute form

---

## Option B: Log in to the Dispute Portal

1. Navigate to the Dispute portal.

2. Sign in:
   - Enter mobile number and password if you have an account.

OR

- Select **Register Now!** to create an account.

3. After login, select:

```text
Dispute > Raise New Dispute
```

---

## Select the Dispute Type

Select the relevant dispute type:

- Account Details  
- Personal Details  
- Credit Enquiry

---

## Update Incorrect Information

1. Update incorrect fields.

2. Click **Submit**.

> You can follow the same process to correct information in:
> - Personal Details  
> - Loan Application Enquiry Details

A pop-up with requested corrections and an option to add bank details as per:

```text
RBI/2023-24/72
DoR.FIN.REC.48/20.16.003/2023-24
```

will be available.

---

## Review and Submit

1. Review requested corrections.

2. Click **Submit**.

---

# What Next?

After submission:

- You will receive an acknowledgement email via the email ID provided.

- Your request is forwarded to the lender for verification.

- Updates may take up to **30 days**.

- Track dispute status using:

```text
Track Disputes
```

available in the header of the Dispute portal.

# Disclaimer

- The data in the Experian Credit Report is based on information reported by banks and financial institutions. The concerned credit granting organizations own the data in your credit report.

- Experian is not authorized to change data in the credit report without written consent from the credit granting organization.

- Experian communicates with the credit granting organization on your behalf to correct errors.

- Final decisions on updating information lie with the credit granting organization.
